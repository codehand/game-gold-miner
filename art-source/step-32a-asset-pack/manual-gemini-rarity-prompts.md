# Gemini Manual Prompts — Elevator Rarity Families

## How to use

- The five visual tiers are `N` gray, `R` green, `SR` blue, `SSR` purple, and `UR` gold.
- For every `shaftElevator` prompt, attach `public/assets/step-32a/elevator-cabin-v2.png` as the reference image.
- For every `surfaceElevatorTower` prompt, attach `public/assets/step-32a/elevator-tower-v2.png` as the reference image.
- Generate one candidate per prompt. Do not ask Gemini for a four-image grid.
- The rarity color is an accent and material signal, not a full-object tint.
