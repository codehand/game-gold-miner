# Elon — regeneration prompts (sharpness pass)
#
# Context for this pass, measured from the checkout:
# - public/assets/marketplace/elon.png is a byte-identical copy of
#   processed/idle-1.png (md5 05adeeb523a63e639af2e37d4f4e0aa6).
# - In that 128x128 file the subject is only 57x92 px (pipeline-meta.json:
#   cell_size 128, fit_scale 0.82, shared_scale true, output_size [57, 92]).
# - .market-portrait img renders it at 115x125 CSS px, so on a DPR 2-3 phone
#   the browser upscales it 1.8x-2.7x. Nothing sets image-rendering.
# - The generator's own cell held 334x541 px of subject, i.e. ~5.9x the shipped
#   pixels: most of the loss happens in the 128 px export, not in generation.
# - Elon's source cell is the most detail-dense of the four portraits
#   (53,640 unique colours, interior 3x3 std 15.03, p90 50.16) vs Cipher's
#   40,015 / 8.22 / 26.58 — which is why it degrades first when shrunk.
#
# So: Prompt A raises pixels-per-frame and strips detail that cannot survive the
# downscale; Prompt B is the real fix for the marketplace card (a dedicated
# large portrait instead of reusing a 128 px runtime cell).
#
# Background note: both prompts use plain pure white, matching the template you
# supplied. If the sheet still goes through the existing generate2dsprite pass
# (threshold 100, magenta key), replace "plain pure white" with
# "perfectly flat #FF00FF" in whatever you paste.
#
# Identity is fixed by reference/elevator-cargo-cat-ssr-elon-design-reference.png
# and the palette was read from it: silver-white fur #d8d8f0 / #f0f0f0,
# cool gray stripes #a8a8c0 / #90a8c0, icy eyes and cube glow #d8f0f0,
# royal violet #6030a8 / #9048d8, gold trim #f0c060, outline near-black navy.

=== PROMPT A — runtime sheet, 2x2 x 4 idle frames (square canvas) ===

Output a square image with a 1 to 1 aspect ratio, at the largest square resolution the model supports, 1024 by 1024 or larger, so that each of the four cells is a square of at least 512 by 512 pixels. Draw a 2D game sprite sheet laid out as a 2 by 2 grid, four frames in total, read left to right along the top row and then left to right along the bottom row. Every frame shows the same character, Elon, a cute chibi cat elevator cargo steward, with a big round head, a short rounded upright body and short legs, silver-white fur with a pale lavender cast, broad cool blue-gray tabby stripes on the head, back, limbs and tail, triangular ears, a small lavender-gray nose, a tiny mouth, big round icy pale-cyan eyes each with a single white highlight dot, a long silver striped tail, a high ceremonial collar in deep royal violet with restrained pale-gold moon-phase trim and an oval faceted amethyst-purple jewel at the throat, and a royal-violet and indigo short cloak. He stands upright and holds one compact sealed celestial cargo cube securely with both front paws against his chest; the cube glows icy pale cyan and carries only abstract sun, moon, orbit and star glyphs, never any readable writing, numerals, equations, logos or brand marks. He is seen from a front three-quarter angle, facing the viewer, standing on an invisible ground line. The four frames form one single restrained idle loop, evenly spaced in time, so that frame 4 leads straight back into frame 1 as a seamless loop; only the eyelids, the ear tips, the tail tip and a slight body bob change between frames, while the paws, the position of the cube and the position of the feet stay fixed. Frame 1 is an alert neutral pose with open eyes; frame 2 is a tiny breathing lift with the eyes softly closed; frame 3 is the neutral pose with a slight ear and tail settle; frame 4 is a tiny breathing settle with the eyes softly closed. The character must be identical in every frame: same fur colours, same stripes, same accessories, same outline thickness, same proportions, same camera distance, same standing scale and the same size. All four frames must be different poses, none of them repeated. Every frame stands on the same invisible ground line so the feet line up across the whole sheet. The whole character, including head, ears, tail, cloak, paws, collar, cube, glow and outline, stays inside the central 76% of every cell with a clear empty margin on all four sides, and nothing crosses a cell boundary. Draw it in a bold cartoon vector art style with a thick dark navy outline of even weight on every shape, inner and outer, exactly two flat value bands per material and no third tint, and a cool silver-white, icy-cyan, royal-violet and pale-gold palette, with no gradients, no gradient meshes, no airbrushing, no ambient occlusion, no texture and no line thickness variation, and with every colour region fully flat, fully opaque and at least 6 pixels wide at a 512 pixel cell. The stripes, the moon-phase trim and the cube glyphs must be large simple shapes: no more than five stripes per limb and per tail, each at least 6 pixels wide at a 512 pixel cell, and no fine hatching, fur strands, dithering, grain or pixel-scale noise anywhere. Keep the whole sheet readable as a clean mobile game sprite whose silhouette and any single frame still read when shrunk to 96 pixels tall; simplify shapes rather than decorate them. Edges must be crisp: the outline is a clean solid band of even width with no smudged, feathered or blurred edge, and no soft halo bleeding into the background. The background must be plain pure white, not cream and not grey, with no floor, no horizon, no vignette and no isometric tile. Do not draw any shadow under or behind the character. Do not draw panel borders, grid lines, frames, numbers, labels, text or a watermark. Do not add any props or decorations that were not asked for, and no detached stars, sparkles, particles, orbs or glow: all light stays inside the cube. Do not add extra cats, scenery, a rarity badge, UI, a signature, human likeness, rockets or vehicles, and do not crop any part of the character.
