# Baron — regeneration prompts v2 (sharpness pass)
#
# Same recipe as the Elon v2 pass (…/elevator-cargo-cat/ssr/elon/prompt-v2-crisp.txt).
#
# Why, measured from the checkout:
# - public/assets/marketplace/baron.png is a byte-identical copy of
#   processed/idle-1.png (md5 8d5bd0f4164242dd5ab2d44263ced229).
# - Subject in that 128x128 file is 66x90 px (pipeline-meta.json: cell_size 128,
#   fit_scale 0.82, shared_scale true, scale_strategy preserve, align feet,
#   output_size [66, 90], paste_position [32, 31]).
# - .market-portrait img renders it at 115x125 CSS px, so on a DPR 2-3 phone the
#   browser upscales it 1.8x-2.7x. Nothing sets image-rendering.
# - The generator cell held 390x528 px of subject, ~5.9x the shipped pixels:
#   most of the loss is the 128 px export, not the generation.
# - Of the four portraits Baron keeps the most pixels and the highest 3x3 interior
#   variance (146.27 lap-std), so it degrades last; v2 is for consistency and for
#   the fluff problem below, not because Baron is the worst offender.
#
# The specific risk for Baron: "fluffy long-haired cream cat" invites the model to
# draw fine fur strands, which turn to mush at 66x90 px. Both prompts below force
# the fluff to be expressed as large scalloped silhouette lobes instead.
#
# Palette (read from the design reference and the shipped frame):
# fur cream #f0d8c0, ivory #f4ead5, gold #f4bd3e, royal blue #32539b,
# sapphire #2a4ea8, cyan highlight #7fd4e8, outline near-black navy #161b33.
# The reference still carries purple #6e4ba2 from the user's concept; the SR
# adaptation shifts that cloth to sapphire/royal blue, so do NOT "correct" it back
# to the project's violet SR key #8a5cd6. Baron's SR read comes from the blue cloth
# plus gold trim plus the one signature scroll, and the ladder allows no full cape.
#
# Background: plain pure white per the supplied template. If this still runs
# through the existing generate2dsprite pass (threshold 100, magenta key), replace
# "plain pure white" with "perfectly flat #FF00FF" in whatever you paste.

=== PROMPT A — runtime sheet, 2x2 x 4 idle frames (square canvas) ===

Output a square image with a 1 to 1 aspect ratio, at the largest square resolution the model supports, 1024 by 1024 or larger, so that each of the four cells is a square of at least 512 by 512 pixels. Draw a 2D game sprite sheet laid out as a 2 by 2 grid, four frames in total, read left to right along the top row and then left to right along the bottom row. Every frame shows the same character, Baron, a cute chibi fluffy long-haired cream cat warehouse manager with a big rounded head, soft cheek tufts, a short stocky body and short limbs, pointed ears with soft tufts, huge round pale-gold eyes each with a single white highlight dot, a small pink nose, a tiny mouth, and a long plume-like cream tail. His fur is cream and ivory, and the fluff must be drawn as large scalloped silhouette lobes along the cheeks, chest, tail and haunches, never as fine individual fur strands. He wears formal ivory-and-gold ceremonial manager armour: a layered chest plate with one shoulder piece, restrained gold trim on exactly two elements, a rich sapphire-blue and royal-blue sash, belt and side cloth panels with restrained pale-cyan highlights, and no full cape. He holds one ornate golden inventory scroll close against his torso, angled diagonally across it like a warehouse ledger or clipboard; the scroll carries only abstract ruled bars and a simple seal shape, never any legible writing, letters, numerals or logos. He is seen from a front three-quarter angle, facing the viewer, standing upright with both feet flat on an invisible ground line. The four frames form one single restrained idle loop, evenly spaced in time, so that frame 4 leads straight back into frame 1 as a seamless loop; only the eyelids, the ear tips, the tail tip, one paw on the scroll, and a slight body bob change between frames, while the pose of the scroll and the position of the feet stay fixed. Frame 1 is an attentive neutral pose with open eyes; frame 2 adds a gentle breath and a soft blink; frame 3 adds a tiny ear and tail settle with a small paw adjustment on the scroll; frame 4 returns toward neutral with another soft blink. The character must be identical in every frame: same fur colours, same fluff lobes, same armour, same scroll, same outline thickness, same proportions, same camera distance, same standing scale and the same size. All four frames must be different poses, none of them repeated. Every frame stands on the same invisible ground line so the feet line up across the whole sheet. The whole character, including head, ears, tail, armour, paws, scroll and outline, stays inside the central 76% of every cell with a clear empty margin on all four sides, and nothing crosses a cell boundary. Draw it in a bold cartoon vector art style with a thick dark navy outline of even weight on every shape, inner and outer, exactly two flat value bands per material and no third tint, and a cream, ivory, gold, royal-blue and sapphire palette using cream #f0d8c0, ivory #f4ead5, gold #f4bd3e, royal blue #32539b, sapphire #2a4ea8, cyan highlight #7fd4e8 and a near-black navy outline, with no gradients, no gradient meshes, no airbrushing, no ambient occlusion, no texture and no line thickness variation, and with every colour region fully flat, fully opaque and at least 6 pixels wide at a 512 pixel cell. The fluff lobes, the armour trim and the scroll marks must be large simple shapes, each at least 8 pixels wide at a 512 pixel cell, with no fine fur strands, no hatching, no dithering, no grain and no pixel-scale noise anywhere. Do not add a rim glow, bloom or soft light halo; if a rim light is wanted, it is one flat solid lighter band of even width running along the upper-left contour only. Keep the whole sheet readable as a clean mobile game sprite whose silhouette and any single frame still read when shrunk to 96 pixels tall; simplify shapes rather than decorate them. Edges must be crisp: the outline is a clean solid band of even width with no smudged, feathered or blurred edge. The background must be plain pure white, not cream and not grey, with no floor, no horizon, no vignette and no isometric tile. Do not draw any shadow under or behind the character. Do not draw panel borders, grid lines, frames, numbers, labels, text or a watermark. Do not add any props or decorations that were not asked for, and no floating coins, keys, gems, stars, sparkles, particles, dust or detached effects. Do not add extra cats, scenery, a rarity badge, UI, a signature, human likeness, and do not crop any part of the character.
